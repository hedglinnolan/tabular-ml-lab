# Part A — three product-owner rulings, made after he opened your prototype

**A1 · The bound of 5 ships.** Unchanged. Your derivation is accepted as a derivation — the median
of sixteen, firing on the tail rather than the typical table — and **your caveat survives the
ruling**: the fixtures are synthetic, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §05 says a lesson learned on synthetic
data is a hypothesis until a real dataset has seen it, and `BOUND` stays a parameter every caller
may pass. Nothing to build; it is recorded so it is not re-argued.

**A2 · The one-card remainder IS special-cased — collapse only when the overflow is two or more.
This overrules you, and your objection was well-made, so here is why it does not bind.**

The recorded lesson is *prefer a rule with no free parameter over a rule with a tuned one*, and the
scroll rule it comes from was tuned to **dataset size** — right at one size, wrong at the next.
**Overflow-of-one is not tuned to anything.** It is the point where the affordance costs exactly one
row to hide exactly one row, so the control returns nothing for the space it occupies. That is a
derivation from what the affordance *is*, not a constant fitted to a corpus, and it does not move
when the data does.

**Say that where it is implemented**, because the next reader meets the recorded lesson before they
meet this exception, and an exception with no stated reason is how a lesson gets quietly reversed.

**A3 · Dismissing or deferring a pushed card frees its slot, and a collapsed finding is promoted
into the vacancy.** The stack keeps its full budget of live findings. `GUIDED-154` closes.

**And you named the risk correctly, so here is the half the ruling creates.** A card the user did not
ask for appears exactly where they cleared one, and the honest failure mode is that it reads as *my
dismissal did not work*.

**The answer is not motion.** This one genuinely *is* a consequence rather than disclosure — the
criterion §05.2's closed list actually uses, so it would qualify where the expand did not — **and
the list stays closed at four.** The app has no mechanism for animating a change of content, a fifth
slot pulls in `GUIDED-073`, and that is deliberately unbuilt.

**So the promoted card is marked, not moved.** A state on the card saying it arrived because a slot
opened — chips and attached warnings, the design language's own vocabulary — which costs no motion
slot and answers the exact question a confused user would ask. It is §09's recorded-absence rule from
the other side: **an object appearing without explanation is as unexplained as one vanishing without
it.**
