# Part A — the debt: selection reaches the fit *and* the page

`GUIDED-095`'s remainder, and `DRIVE-011` closes with it.

The class is *every decision recorded to be fitted inside the training fold is read by nothing
that fits.* `pipeline_plan` closed it for the missingness declarations, the recipe variants and
the deferred transforms. **Feature selection is still open, and it is inside the class rather
than adjacent to it:** `selection.declare` takes `scope=TRAIN_FOLDS` by default, refuses any
third option, and says in its own refusal that *there is no option that fits on the whole
table.* Nothing fits it. A recorded scope nothing enforces is the same defect as a recorded
strategy nothing fits, one object over.

**Both halves, in one part, and that is the point of putting it first.** `DRIVE-011`: the
Features selection step has no interface at all — `choose_selection` is served and
`set_selection` is never called from the page. So building the executor alone would ship a
capability whose only caller is a test, in the loop immediately after `LOOP.md` §05 was amended
to forbid exactly that. **This part is the first test of that rule and it should be treated as
one.** A user picks a method and a count on the page; the selector is fitted inside each fold;
the run says how many features survived and names them.

**Fold-local means fold-local.** Selection fitted once on the training rows and reused across
folds is the weaker claim `scope=TRAIN_ROWS` exists to let a project *say*. If you cannot honor
`TRAIN_FOLDS` for a method, record `TRAIN_ROWS` and say so — never claim the stronger scope.

# Part B — Explain, scoped to what this repository can already defend

**Read `GUIDED-101` before you start. It is a scoping instruction, not a complaint.**

The four research packs contain **zero** explainability content — the grep across 3,602 lines
returns nothing substantive, and `FEATURE_PARITY.md` has no Explain row. Every other step of
this journey rests on a sourced section; this one rests on nothing. So you are **not** being
asked to decide what the field holds about SHAP versus permutation importance. That is
`LOOP.md` §08's new-construction-under-a-governing-rule, and it is design work.

**Build the narrow, defensible slice:**

1. **Permutation importance, computed on the held-out rows**, using `sklearn.inspection`. It is
   sklearn-native, needs no absent dependency, and the held-out choice is the one with a
   leakage consequence — importance computed on training rows is the model grading its own
   homework, exactly as a calibration curve on training predictions is.
2. **Identity from the lockbox labels, never from positions.** `MINE-014` is `critical` and
   `OPEN`: Classic's explainability re-derives the test set *positionally* from a frame that
   may have changed since training. Decision A's barrier exists so this door does not have to.
   `MINE-014` stays `OPEN` for Classic — note it `resolved-in-core; closes at L11 convergence
   of pages/07`, verbatim, per §09.
3. **Read core's prose, do not compose your own.** `ml/plot_narrative.py` already holds
   `interpretation_permutation_importance()` — *"Importance = drop in metric when the feature
   is shuffled… Sklearn/Altmann et al."* — and `narrative_permutation_importance(perm_data,
   model_name)` takes exactly sklearn's output shape. `ml/publication.py:1213` holds the methods
   sentence. A Guided Explain step that writes its own sentences beside these is `AUDIT-008`
   arriving in the last step of the journey.

**And the thing worth building this part for, which came out of the register rather than out of
me.** Every transform in the Features catalogue carries `explainability_cost`, and

# Part C — Report: the transcript becomes the manuscript

The thesis, tested for the first time: *the transcript the user scrolls and the manuscript they
export are the same object at two levels of formality.*

`turbotab/draft.py` is the foundation and already works — it folds recorded decisions into
sections and marks `AUTHOR_GAP` (`[AUTHOR REQUIRED]`) where the app cannot know something. The
page fetches `/draft` today and renders `{"paragraphs": []}`.

**The specific thing L35 made possible, and it is this part's deadline.** `GUIDED-089`'s note
says `draft.py` has no reference to missingness or preprocess, so the recorded methods sentence
was never exported — and until L35 that was *fortunate*, because the sentence disagreed with the
fit. It agrees now. **So the preprocessing plan reaches the methods section in this part, and it
is only safe to do because Part B of L35 landed.** Say that in the commit; it is the clearest
example this project has of why sequencing was not a delay.

**What Report is and is not, scoped so it does not become L10.** It is the Guided *step*: it
assembles what has been recorded — target, grain, eligibility, the seal and its basis,
preprocessing, features, the fitted models with their held-out numbers, the divergences, the
figures flagged for the manuscript, and the limitations the record already carries. It is **not**
the checklist engine (TRIPOD+AI, STROBE-nut), which is L10 and absorbs the domain track.

**Two rules it must not break.** `AUDIT-001` is `FIXED` and lives next door — the generated
manuscript once reported a raw *p* < 0.05 count and named no correction. Nothing this part emits
may assert a statistical claim the record does not hold. And where the app cannot know
something, `AUTHOR_GAP` is the answer, never a plausible sentence: an exported methods section
that reads smoothly and states something nobody decided is the governing rule's worst failure,
because it is the artifact that leaves the building.

`stale_downstream` is written by `project.py:662`, serialized by `archive.py`, counted by
`devchecks.py`, and **rendered by nothing.**

**The fix is a renderer and a veil, not a recompute, and not a delete.** `PRODUCT_VISION.md`
principle 4: the past is editable, never silently destroyed — changes mark downstream work
stale, *visible, veiled, recoverable*. Do not resolve this by clearing the run.
`DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §05 rule 3 is **propagate**, and **§05.2 is new — read it.** It names
identity continuity as what the motion is *for*, and closes the list of places it may be spent
at exactly four, of which propagate is one. So the veiled run must be the **same object**
wearing a stale state, not a replaced one — and §05.2 also records that no mechanism for
animating a change of content currently exists, so if the veil is a repaint, say so rather than
claiming a motion the page cannot perform.

**And the scope is wider than the run now**, which is why this is Part D rather than a footnote:
after Part A and B there are three downstream artifacts — the fitted run, the selection, the
explanation. Ask of each what upstream change invalidates it. A staleness renderer that knows
about one of the three is this loop's own defect class.
