Does the motion need partial updates?

The question GUIDED-073 asks. Switch between the two assay captures a few times in each render mode — fast enough that a switch lands inside the previous 250 ms settle — then read the counters. Tab into a cell first to test focus.

The premise is measured and does not need a browser: that switch changes 6 of 16 cells — power on all four models and scale on the two with requires_scaled_numeric. A wholesale repaint rebuilds all 24 nodes to move 6. The consequence does need one, which is what the counters below are for: a destroyed node cannot finish an animation and cannot hold a focus ring, and transitioncancel does not fire on removal — so the repaint mode cannot even report what it broke, and the count is derived by subtraction.
