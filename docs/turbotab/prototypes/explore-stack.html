The Explore stack, at three bounds

Every loop before this one was gated by a command. This one is gated by you opening it. ROADMAP.md condition 7 says the engine must be correct, must surface, and must be beautiful — and the third has no automated instrument at all. pageharness.py says so in its own docstring: it proves what the controller renders and cannot prove that anything is visible. So this page is the substitute, and the only question it asks is how many findings the Explore section should push before it collapses the rest.

Bound

Why two is on this page, and why it is not the answer

PRODUCT_VISION.md §08 asks “how many noticings may a step raise at once?” and answers two. That number is right, and it answers a different question. Two is the interruption budget — how often the coach may take you off what you were doing. The Explore stack interrupts nothing; it is the section, opening already answered. Press 2 above and look at the clinical table: ten of thirteen findings go behind one affordance, and five of the eight are the ones the clinical lens exists to produce. That is the domain content three loops made reachable, deleted from view by a number borrowed from a different question.

What the bound is, and what it is not allowed to do

Three things it may never do, all ruled elsewhere. Nothing that gates a decision is collapsed — ROADMAP.md Decision B, “a blocker that only offers is not gating”, and criticals sit outside the bound entirely. The shelf is never shortened — everything served is still here, one press away, in the same payload. The remainder is counted and typed — “7 more — 3 warnings, 4 cautions” is an answer; a fade-out is a truncation wearing an affordance’s clothes.

The five tables

Real payloads, captured by driving the API against the fixtures in turbotab/sample_data/ and partitioned by the real turbotab/attention.py. Nothing here is typed by hand. Each stack renders exactly as the Guided page renders it; press the affordance to open the remainder, which is what the app does — instantly, with no fetch and no animation (DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md §05.2: an expand is disclosure, not consequence).

The case this page exists to have you rule on. At a bound of five, nhanes_kilojoules.csv has six findings, so the affordance hides one card and costs one row. That is mildly silly and it is not wrong. The obvious fix — collapse unless the overflow is one — is a second tuned parameter, and DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md §05 has already paid for that shape once: the scroll rule was revised to a size-dependent condition, was correct at one dataset size and wrong at the next, and the lesson written down is “prefer a rule with no free parameter over a rule with a tuned one.” So it ships without the special case, and this panel is here so the decision is yours rather than the builder’s.

What is not on this page.

And the honest limit on the whole argument: the bound is the median of this repository’s sixteen fixtures, which are synthetic. DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md §05 is explicit that a lesson learned on synthetic data is a hypothesis until a real dataset has seen it. The number is therefore a stated parameter with a stated reason, not a constant — turbotab/attention.py’s BOUND, and every caller may pass another.
