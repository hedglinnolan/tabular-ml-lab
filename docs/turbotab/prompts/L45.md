# Part A — the ordering, and the ruling you asked for

**A1 · The critical is buried, and this is a routing-constitution violation rather than a design
preference.** Measured by the adjudicator on `clinical_labs.csv` under the clinical lens:

The server ranks correctly. The `critical` finding — *`glucose` holds two populations a factor of 18
apart*, a mixed-unit predictor — is **`rank=0`, first of twenty-one.** The page then renders
`bySource("profile").concat(bySource("pack"))`, **nothing anywhere sorts**, and the five profile
findings carry ranks 13, 17, 18, 19 and 20. So the five *least* consequential findings render above
the eight most consequential, and **the only `critical` lands at card six of thirteen.**

`ROADMAP.md` Decision B, verbatim: *"Blockers rank first. A blocker third in a list of nine is a
blocker in name only. Ordering is part of the gate."*

**The collision is real and here is the ruling.** L42 grouped profile-then-pack for a good stated
reason — *the profile speaks about the table and the pack speaks about the field, and a reader who
cannot tell which is which has lost the thing the lens was for.* That reason is sound and the
remedy was wrong. **Ranking wins; grouping becomes a label, not an order.** One list in the
server's rank order, with each card carrying its source as a chip — which is the design language's
own move (attributes as chips, warnings attached to what they warn about) and satisfies both goals
while violating neither ruling.

**A2 · `AUDIT-030` — ruled, so the revert can be finished.** You reverted it because fixing it
turned a green test red and adjudicating that meant deciding what the manuscript should say. Here is
the decision; the row carries it in full.

The word `validation` is **false** — there is no validation split — so `GUIDED-104`'s
self-correcting note does not apply, because that was accepted only where the weaker claim was still
true. The manuscript says two things instead: **what was compared** (the models were ranked on the
held-out set) and **what that costs** (selecting among N by held-out score makes the chosen model's
reported performance optimistic by an unquantified amount). **Unquantified is the honest word** —
§A5.5 asks for optimism correction, this door computes none, so state the direction and decline the
number, exactly as the annotation box declines a not-estimable one.

The test is **trap #3c**: it pins the false word. Its *name* is the property worth keeping; rewrite
the assertion.

`AUDIT-031` and `AUDIT-032` stay reverted and open — they are not this loop.

# Part B — `GUIDED-149` · the budget

**Twenty-one findings on one clinical table. The prototype settled on two. Both of those numbers are
right, and they are answers to different questions** — this is the distinction the loop turns on and
`PRODUCT_VISION.md` §08 conflates them.

- The **interruption budget** is about *noticings that interrupt at a decision point.* Two. That is
  the Clippy problem and §04 already answers it.
- The **stack** is §04's *"the section opens already answered — a ranked stack of findings, each
  carrying its plot, its plain-language consequence, and its downstream action."* A stack is not an
  interruption, and **capping it at two would delete the domain content three loops just made
  reachable.** Do not do that.

**What governs the stack is ranking, bounding and disclosure — never truncation.** Three rules, all
already ruled elsewhere:

1. **Nothing that gates a decision is ever collapsed.** Decision B's third clause: a blocker is
   always pushed, never offered, and *a blocker that only offers is not gating.* Criticals rank
   first and are outside the bound.
2. **The shelf is never shortened.** Everything remains reachable. Judgment renders as ranking and
   disclosure, never as absence.
3. **The remainder is counted and typed, never merely hidden.** §09's recorded-absence rule and the
   no-silent-caps rule together: *"9 more — 6 formatting, 3 distribution"* is an answer; a fade-out
   is a truncation wearing an affordance's clothes.

**The bound itself is the one number I am not giving you**, because it is the product owner's to
look at — that is Part C. Build it as a **stated parameter with a stated reason**, not a magic
constant, and make Part C able to show it at more than one value.

**The expand is instant, and this is ruled** — `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §05.2, where the ruling now
lives. The motion list is closed at four, an expand is *disclosure rather than consequence*, §05
rule 5 governs, and the app has no mechanism for animating a change of content anyway. **Do not
spend a fifth motion slot and do not reach for `startViewTransition`** — that is `GUIDED-073`'s
stack decision and it is deliberately unbuilt.

# Part D — the probe: the extremes

A bounded list lies at its edges, and every edge here is reachable by a real project.

**Drive the stack at:** zero findings · one · exactly the bound · one over · twenty-one · all
`critical` · no `critical` · a lens with no pack. For each, assert what a person sees and that the
counted remainder's arithmetic is **exact** — the number in the affordance equals the number behind
it, in every case. A count that is off by one at an edge is the app asserting something false about
its own contents.

**And two properties that must hold at every size**, asserted rather than eyeballed: **no `critical`
is ever inside the collapsed group**, and **the total rendered plus the total collapsed equals the
total served.** Both are one-liners and both are the kind of thing that silently stops being true.

**Report the counts**: sizes driven, the bound, criticals ever collapsed (must be zero), and any
size where rendered + collapsed ≠ served.
