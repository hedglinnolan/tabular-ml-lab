**Three harness facts that cost me two wrong answers. All three are real; none is documented in
`pageharness.py`:**

1. `document.querySelectorAll("[id]")` returns **nothing**, and `document.body.innerHTML` is **empty**.
   A reader built on either reports *every* question as unrendered. **Collect explicit ids** — harvest
   them from the markup with `re.findall(r'\bid="([A-Za-z0-9_-]+)"', html)`, which found **135**
   *(re-derived at `d66ce3b`)*.
2. `__harness.matches(el, sel)` is a **predicate, not a query**. Use `document.querySelectorAll`.
3. **Assert your reader read something before believing any absence.** Mine collects >100k chars on a
   healthy render; the guard is `assert len(blob) > 5000`.
