**C1 · Sentinel codes in a bounded block — §B1.1 calls this "the highest-yield check in this pack."**

what makes the finding actionable rather than a warning.

**C2 · `GUIDED-135` (file it) — the reverse-coding audit, §B1.2.** The app **already asks**:

Build §B1.2's **reverse-coding audit table**: item | text (truncated) | item–rest *r* (raw) |
