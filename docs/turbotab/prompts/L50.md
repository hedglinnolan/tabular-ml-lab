# Part B — genomics: what your numbers are

**The pack calls this its single most valuable artifact, in those words.** Source:
`research/GENOMICS_PACK.md` **§02, "Data-type detection — the highest-leverage diagnostic in the
pack"** — read that section first and build from it, not from recollection.

**Nine signatures, and the table is written out.** Per column: sum, min, max, median, % zeros, %
integer. Per matrix: global max, negatives present. The classifications are **raw counts · estimated
counts · CPM/TPM (indistinguishable, and say so) · TMM- or median-of-ratios-scaled CPM · FPKM/RPKM ·
VST · rlog · microarray log2 intensity · already log-ratio or z-scored.**

**The hard rule is `SETTLED` and it is one line:** any negative value rules out raw counts, CPM, TPM
and FPKM. Assert it as a rule, not as a heuristic.

**The branched coaching is written and every branch is `SETTLED`** — DESeq2's own words that *"only
the count values allow assessing the measurement precision correctly"*; that TPM/CPM/FPKM close off
the negative-binomial route because count-level variance is destroyed, and **feeding them to a count
model runs silently while its p-values are wrong**; that FPKM is not comparable across samples *even
in principle* (Wagner, Kim & Lynch 2012) and that total-count and RPKM normalization did not control
the false-positive rate where TMM and median-of-ratios did (Dillies 2013); that VST and rlog are for
visualization and **never** the input to a DE test; that microarray means the count toolchain does
not apply.

**And the capability matrix is the part that is not a detector.** The pack specifies a card showing
**classification, confidence, the evidence that drove it, and which downstream steps are now enabled,
disabled, or require input.** That is one new component and it is the only thing in this loop that
discovers a shape — **build it once, narrow, and let the nine signatures be the wide part.**

**`GUIDED-120`'s lesson applies to the whole of B**: a rule tuned on one fixture is a hypothesis. See
Part F on fixtures, because eight of the nine signatures currently have nothing to fire on.

# Part C — genomics: gene IDs, and the one that is measured

**Source: `research/GENOMICS_PACK.md` §01, "Gene IDs".** Four diagnostics, all fill-out:

- **version suffixes present** — joins against unversioned annotation fail silently, dropping genes
- **duplicate IDs after symbol mapping** — many-to-one
- **mixed vocabularies**
- **Excel corruption** — date-like strings `1-Mar`, `2-Sep`, `44621`

**Excel corruption is `SETTLED` and it is measured**: Ziemann, Eren & El-Osta (*Genome Biology*
17:177, 2016) found gene-name conversion errors in **~20% of papers** with supplementary Excel gene
lists, and Abeysooriya et al. (*PLoS Comput Biol* 2021) found the rate had **risen**. HGNC renamed
`SEPT*`→`SEPTIN*` and `MARCH*`→`MARCHF*` partly because of it.

> **Never auto-repair — report and stop.**

That is `DOMAIN_SCIENCE.md` §01's **hard-stop class**, and the app already models it: a finding with a
proposed action and **no pre-selection**. Do not invent a new component for it.

# Part D — metabolomics: the three families a generic tool cannot do

**Source: `research/METABOLOMICS_PACK.md` §01** — read *"Sample-role detection"*, *"Run order, batch,
and design"* and *"Value-state diagnostics"*. **This is the widest part of the loop and the regex
library is written out in full.** Do not compress it.

**D1 · Sample roles.** Case-insensitive, applied to sample names **and** to any metadata column named
`type`, `sample_type`, `role`, `class`, `group`. Six role families: pooled QC, dilution QC, blanks,
system suitability, calibrants/standards, and the proteomics set. The patterns are in the pack.

**And the coaching for the absent case is the valuable half**, because it is what a reviewer expects:
*"I couldn't find any pooled QC samples… Without them I can't compute QC-RSD, D-ratio, or drift
correction. If QCs were run but aren't in this file, add them now; they cannot be reconstructed
later."* That sentence is the pack's, and it is a **refusal with a stated reason** — the kind
`DOMAIN_SCIENCE.md` §03b says a reviewer notices.

**D2 · Run order, batch and design.** Detect `injection.order`, `inj_order`, `run_order`, `sequence`,
`acq_order`, `AcquisitionDateTime`, `batch`, `plate`, `well`, `position`, `plex`, `TMT.channel`,
`polarity`; derive run order from a timestamp where one exists. **If no run order exists, say so
loudly** — half the downstream diagnostics become impossible and the pack says so.

Also detect **subject ID**, and note the pack's own line: repeated subject IDs are *"routinely
missed"*, and the coaching — *"48 samples from 24 subjects. Treating these as 48 independent
observations would roughly double your apparent sample size"* — is the **grain question** arriving
through a second door. **Route it to the grain answer the lockbox already holds; do not ask twice.**

**D3 · Value states.** Zeros and NAs mean different things **and vendors disagree** — XCMS
`fillPeaks` writes small numbers, MZmine writes 0, MaxQuant writes 0 for *not quantified*, Progenesis
writes 0. **Ask; never default.** Already-transformed detection: negatives present, or a max below
~40 with a positive min and low dynamic range, or column means ≈ 0. **Warn hard — a second log
transform is a silent catastrophe.** Dynamic range: raw untargeted intensities span 10²–10⁹, and a
ratio below 10² means something has already been done. Plus duplicate feature/sample IDs, all-zero
features, all-zero samples, constant features, and the ion-mode split.

# Part E — metabolomics: redundancy, the pack's stated differentiator

**Source: `research/METABOLOMICS_PACK.md` §01, "Redundancy detection — a real differentiator".**

Untargeted features are not independent: adducts (`[M+H]+`, `[M+Na]+`, `[M+K]+`, `[M+NH4]+`,
`[M-H]-`, `[M+HCOO]-`), isotopologues (Δm/z **1.00336**), dimers, in-source fragments. Cluster by
near-identical RT (**±0.05–0.1 min**) **and** inter-feature correlation (**r > 0.9**), and report an
estimated **effective feature count**.

**The sentence this produces is the whole point:** if 5,000 features collapse to ~1,200 clusters,
the user's *"5,000 metabolites"* claim is wrong by roughly **4×** — and that number lands in the
manuscript's data-description sentence and in the multiple-testing denominator.

**Deliberately a different shape from D**: D reads names and dtypes, E computes over the matrix. That
is the two-builds-of-different-shape rule doing its job inside a fill-out loop.
