## 3 · Rulings (the orchestrator, 2026-10-17)

1. **The calibration intercept is calibration-in-the-large:** the mean of y − ŷ, with the slope
   fixed at 1, in outcome units. The slope is the least-squares slope of y on ŷ. This is what the
   engine serves and BEATS_PREDICT reports, and it is the standard definition (Van Calster et al.
   2016, J Clin Epidemiol 74:167; 2019, BMC Med 17:230). The parent's acceptance 2 ("the
   intercept against OLS of y on ŷ") is amended. The reference test already uses the mean of y − ŷ.
2. **The held-out baseline** is the training rows' mean outcome, scored on the held-out rows with
   the same metrics (MSE, RMSE, MAE). It is served in `HoldoutDetail.baseline` and sealed with the
   rest. The held-out verdict applies the cross-validated verdict's rule and floor to the held-out
   R² gain and its served interval, reading the floor from `baseline.min_gain`. Its sentence
   interpolates the floor. No Results number is computed in the browser.
3. **Cancel:** a held fit shows Fit with its estimate and no Cancel, since nothing runs. While the
   fit job runs, the Fit position shows its progress and a Cancel (FOUNDATION §7, "a Cancel where
   Fit was"). The parent's W1-B row ("Cancel when held") is amended. Withdrawing the lock on an
   early Cancel waits for the Estimate lane (W3); under Predict nothing locks.
4. **Results' sweep under Predict is the noticings triage** from `GET /triage`. `set_updating` is
   its own Decide card, not a sweep line (the parent's §2 and §5 are amended).
5. **Binned calibration points wait for W2.** v0 draws the served curve, intercept and slope with
   their intervals. The seam is recorded in `V2X_SEAMS.md`.
6. **A declaration line gets a card, never a blank.** The dispatcher's fallback is GenericAsk, and
   the renderer contract test fails on any `(source, key)` without one.
7. **Readings and single findings** render from what the quest line and the findings artifact
   already serve. Nothing new is served for v0.
8. **Routes:** `/p/:pid` is the shell, and `/p/:pid/classic` is today's screen until W2 closes.
9. **P8 serves the grouping the export bundle uses.** The manuscript rail and the downloaded
   methods can then never differ: TRIPOD+AI sections under Predict, and the bundle's own order
   under Estimate.
10. **The validation scheme is `set_validation`'s,** and the seal's card sends only the draw. If
    the premise (that SealAsk resets the scheme) fails, the card is left as it is, and the report
    says so.
