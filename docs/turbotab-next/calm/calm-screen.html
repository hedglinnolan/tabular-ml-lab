Should people with implausible energy intakes be removed?

A recall far outside a normal day's intake is usually a reporting error. Removing those people changes who your results describe.

Why does this matter?

Fixed cut-offs are the field's habit (Willett, Nutritional Epidemiology, 2013). They are not individualized, so they also remove some real people with very low or high intakes. For an association like this one, the sounder default is to keep everyone and report the screened result beside it as a check (Banna et al. 2017, Front Nutr 4:45).
