How color works in TurboTab

  1. Warm paper is the app; the cool canvas is your data. The temperature tells you which side is talking.
  2. Indigo is the choice you are making. Pointing at an option tints it, picking it fills it, the button records it, and on the canvas indigo marks exactly what the choice touches.
  3. Gray data is your data as it is now. A transformation plays from gray to indigo, step by step, so you watch the choice act.
  4. Status colors are reserved. Green only for recorded, amber only for the coach, red only for a blocker, and each comes with words.
  5. Comparisons use their own five hues, never indigo, ordered so neighbors stay distinct for color-blind readers.
