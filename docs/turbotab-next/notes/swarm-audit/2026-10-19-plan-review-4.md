**Remaining or new flaws, ranked**

1. **The persona rule cannot be computed.** §7: "a persona is kept if its coverage on the Estimate fixture exceeds the range of the three Haiku replicates' coverage for that persona." The persona's coverage on that fixture *is* those three runs; a mean cannot "exceed" its own range. Revision: a persona earns its place when its mean coverage differs from the conventional persona's by more than the larger of the two within-persona ranges, in steps of 1/m. Also, "coverage" names two things in §7 (share of material decisions examined; interval coverage over draws); rename one.

2. **The measurement fixture's generator is new, and "material" is undefined there.** `make_fixtures.metabolomics` (sample_data, line 51) draws `responder` as Bernoulli(0.42) independent of every feature, sets `batch` = run_order ≤ 40 (so batch and drift are one variable) and has no dilution. §5 says "its generator: a known set of truly associated features…"; that generator does not exist and must be written with batch offsets not monotone in run order, or drift correction and batch correction cannot be graded apart. Buildable: the engine's feature-wise family (`models/featurewise.py`) does per-feature least squares with Benjamini–Hochberg under Estimate. But §7 defines material under Estimate as a shift in *the* reported estimate; this fixture reports 392. So the sweep, coverage and "finding what matters" are undefined on it, and discovery quality has no margin (b₀ and r₀ have rules; d₀ does not). Revision: material = a change in the discovery set of at least k features at q < 0.05, k pre-registered; grade effect sizes on the log2 scale only; state that the fixture decides nothing and is reported.

3. **b₀'s terms are ambiguous and its two conditions can conflict.** "Half the smallest bias the fixture plants" should be the population bias from the generator, not the 100 draws' mean; "the oracle plan's bias standard error over the draws" should be the standard error of the mean bias (SD/√100), and a plan passes on its mean bias. The two conditions hold together only if the generator is tuned so half the smallest planted bias ≥ 4 SE; say so in §5 item 5, since package 2 builds the generator before 6b computes b₀. Also say what happens if the Opus reference fails the margins (the fixture is re-examined, not the models).

4. **The unpaired interval has no formula, and time-ordered plans have no 5 × 5.** `corrected_t` exists only paired (validation.py 545). Fold scores within 25 repeated folds are correlated; a naive Welch t over 25 against 25 understates the variance several-fold. Revision: apply the same (1/k + n₂/n₁) inflation to each arm's variance, in `prereg.json`. Separately, under a time-ordered fold scheme the folds "are the same in every repeat" (folds.py `TIME_ORDERED_ONCE`) and the comparison substrate does not run (validation.py 387), so a glucose plan with time-ordered folds yields 5 scores and 4 df. Say the sweep scores every refit with random repeated k-fold within the training rows, whatever the plan chose.

5. **The glucose benchmark: wording and Bang's activity item.** (a) §5 and glucose_benchmarks.md say the model's "predictions thresholded at 126 mg/dL are compared"; an AUC needs the *outcome* thresholded (FPG ≥ 126, no known diabetes) and the continuous prediction ranked; thresholding the prediction gives one operating point. (b) Bang's "physically active" is PAQ520 ("compared with others your age"), discontinued after 2005–06; 2017–March 2020 has the GPAQ items (PAQ605–PAD675). The primary comparison therefore uses Bang's score *adapted*, and the script must declare and quote the mapping for activity and family history (MCQ300C). (c) Yoo 2026 and De Silva 2020 define prediabetes with HbA1c or OGTT; a 100 mg/dL threshold is a different outcome. Call them reference points, not benchmarks.

6. **The hardened Predict fixture breaks the dry run's evidence.** The dry run's run_02 is `clinical_readmission_predict` under the generator §5 item 4 replaces; its `answers.json` truths (C 0.7419, the seven terms) and `FIXTURE_TRUTHS` change with it. Package 2 should regenerate the truths and rerun the three-record dry run, and §TL;DR's "18 checks agree" should name the commit.

7. **Coverage's denominator cannot hold what only the explorer reaches.** Sweeps run around the default and conventional plans; a decision offered only on an explorer's path (an imputation method after choosing to impute) is never material by construction, so the persona comparison is scored on the conventional menu. Acceptable for the pilot if stated; or add the explorer's final plan to the swept set on the Estimate fixture.

8. **Order, counts and dependencies.** Early stopping ("after its persona runs") needs the Estimate fixture to be the first wave; §6 randomizes order within a wave but does not fix the wave order. Whether the trio's three runs are among the 45 is unstated. 6c waits for 3 and 5, unlisted. If the replicates are cut, Haiku's unit becomes 2 of 3; say so. Materiality's "6 of 9" should be of the non-refused plans, with a minimum count.

9. **Prior-driven share's matcher.** "Matches on decision, markers and direction": markers as a set of live-marker names, thresholds ignored, else the share depends on the matcher's strictness. One line in 6d.

**Does the pilot decide what it says?** The production analyst: yes, once flaws 3 and 4 are pinned, the Estimate fixture bites and the rule is scripted.
