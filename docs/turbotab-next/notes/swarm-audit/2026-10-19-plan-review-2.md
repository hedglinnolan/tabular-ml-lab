# Second review of the swarm plan, version 2 (2026-10-19)

An independent Fable 5.1 reviewer at xhigh effort, read-only, given the project's intent in Nolan's words, the first audit and SWARM_PLAN version 2 (ee835478), asked to read it cold and break it where it is still weak. Its report, verbatim:

**TL;DR.** Version 2 is a genuine redesign, not a renamed version 1: it answers the first audit's structure (two kinds of claim, mechanical arms, replay scoring, a scripted pre-registration, a closed loop, a shuffled control). But it is not ready to build as a whole after one more revision, because four load-bearing pieces are still names without numbers or mechanics: "material" is undefined and five rules rest on it; the sweep is unsized and silently depends on replaying a sealed plan into a fresh project; the pilot's claim cycle and shuffled control cannot promote anything on six fully used datasets, so the false-promotion rate it promises is 0/0 and passes silently; and the fixtures as specified will not separate Haiku from Sonnet on decision quality. §10 item 1 (record v2, driver flags, signed change) and the shuffle tool can be built now from code that exists; items 2–4 need one more revision of §5–§9 with the numbers filled in. The driver, the record's discipline, the dry run and the Sullivan sweep remain sound and should be copied, not reopened.

**The audit's findings**

1. Sensitivity, not goodness: **partly.** Surfacing/choice split, sweep, signed Δ are named; plasmode designs still have no design, loss or replicate count; "material" undefined.
2. Slop gate untested: **partly.** Shuffled control and planted effect named; at pilot scale nothing can be promoted, and "false by construction" holds only if the test stage also runs in the shuffled world (below).
3. Pilot cannot see quality: **partly.** Replay scorer, non-null Estimate fixture, refusals as a rate: yes. Regret's loss unspecified; Predict fixture too easy; measurement fixture has no stated purpose, loss or truth.
4. Axes prose only: **answered in design, unstaged.** ~40 markers against the record's seven fields today (`record.py` 161-171, `assemble.regime_of` 1324-1341); Kish's n and the condition index each appear in one engine module; live vs post-hoc markers not separated.
5. Agents' prior untested: **partly.** Default and random arms added; the Bang replication sweep does not exist (`research/replication/` holds only sullivan2021; `notes/replication/bang2009.md` is a source audit) and is not in §10.
6. Pre-registration: **mostly answered**; arithmetic defects remain (below).
7. Exploration for show: **answered** (preview reason, previews sampled, planted reasons, coverage).
8. Closed loop: **answered in design**; the Decide cap has no number and nothing counts Decide-tier questions per journey yet.
9. Sizing: **answered** for agent runs; the sweep's compute is the new unsized item.

**Remaining and new flaws, ranked**

1. **The pilot's claim cycle measures nothing, and the control fails silently.** §9.3 grades a surfacing claim on held-out datasets in its region; the pilot uses all six. §9.5 counts "the share of the control's claims that reach a grade", but CLAIMS_REGISTRY §2.4 lets an author self-grade *judgment*, so every proposal trivially reaches a grade, or none reaches a higher one: either way the rate is uninformative. "False by construction" also needs the whole pipeline, including the held-out test, to run in the shuffled world; a shuffled-proposed claim tested on real data can be truly validated. Revision: define promotion as *replicated* or above; run the test stage inside the shuffled corpus; declare the pilot's cycle a plumbing test with pre-stated outputs (N proposals, zero promotions in both arms, the planted effect's claim proposed and routed); add the statistic that does work at this scale and measures Nolan's exact fear, the **prior-driven share**: the overlap between the theorist's proposals from the real and the shuffled corpus. A true false-promotion rate needs a held-out shelf of generator draws at varied parameters, which is post-pilot.

2. **"Material" is undefined, and coverage (§3), the persona rule, pull, admission and cost-per-result all rest on it.** The dry run's exploring Predict record has 23 decisions and zero with a measured materiality (`dryrun/run_02`, source None for all 23; the second fit's paired movement exists but credits no single decision). Per-fold scores are in the record (`scores.*.folds`, repeats 1), so the signed Δ with the corrected t (`validation.py` 545) is computable post hoc, but with K=5 and one repeat it has 4 df: on the 480-row fixture almost nothing will be material; on Bang almost everything. Revision: in `prereg.json`, define material per purpose in loss units (Predict: the corrected-t interval of Δ excludes zero and |Δ| exceeds a set fraction of the primary score's interval half-width; Estimate: shift of at least half the interval half-width), fix the sweep's repeat count, and report coverage only where at least k decisions are material.

3. **The sweep depends on replay and is unsized.** A sealed project refuses changes reaching the result, so a sweep "around each agent run's final plan" must rebuild that plan in a fresh project: replay on the same data, which §8 calls unproven and §10 orders after the sweep. Cost: Sullivan's linear refits ran 41 s median on 39k rows (`sullivan2021.md` §8); a Predict shelf fit on Bang (31,126 × 54) is unmeasured; around 20–30 offered decisions × 7–10 plans per dataset is hundreds of full-pipeline fits, daytime fan noise for days. Revision: dedupe refits by plan digest; in the pilot sweep around the default plan and one plan per model; exclude readings with one true answer (code-or-amount) from the varied set; size from §6's scripted fit and cap sweep hours in prereg.

4. **The fixtures will not separate the models.** The Predict fixture is a seven-term logistic with one log transform (`answers.json`); any path keeping all predictors sits near the oracle, so regret ≈ 0 for everyone. The measurement fixture (`make_fixtures.metabolomics`: 80 participants, 392 features, `responder`) has no stated purpose, loss or truth, so "Opus scored by replay" on it is undefined. Only the new Estimate fixture can discriminate, and its missingness must be in a covariate, dependent on the observed outcome (MAR), for complete-case to be biased and imputation unbiased; outcome-dependent missingness in the outcome itself leaves no gradable right answer. Revision: harden the Predict redraw (a nonlinearity the default misses, an interaction, a predictor mis-measured at use time); write the measurement fixture's question and truth or drop it from the pilot; pre-register which fixture decides which statistic.

5. **Pre-registration arithmetic.** Opus is one run per fixture, so the "ceiling" is a single noisy point and a bad Opus run passes everyone; "paired differences per fixture" has three pairs; the persona rule's "replicates' spread" comes from three Haiku replicates per cell on two fixtures and is then applied to Sonnet and to real datasets with no replicates; "Sonnet's replicates first" names an arm that does not exist (only Haiku has replicates); "5% for a null's coverage" refers to `survey_support_estimate`, no longer among the six; the trio's Opus run on Bang contradicts "Opus only on fixtures" and the 51 count. Revision: oracle-anchored absolute margins (regret ≤ r₀, |bias| ≤ b₀, coverage within a stated band over ≥20 draws) with Opus as a reference arm; three Opus runs on the Estimate fixture if it is to be a ceiling; a cut order over arms that exist.

6. **Replay is less unproven than §8 says, and differently hard.** `core/export/replay.py` already rebuilds a project from `decisions.jsonl` as a fold of the log in a fresh home ("every stage computes from the answers exactly as recorded") and refuses inputs whose SHA-256 differs. A fresh draw needs that refusal lifted under a flag, a rule for records keyed to findings that may not fire on new bytes (`apply_repair`), and a scorer: I found no route serving predictions on new rows, so regret may have to be Brier against the oracle on each fresh draw's sealed holdout (96–120 rows, noisy). Revision: name the existing module as the base and write the loss.

7. **Live versus post-hoc markers.** A promoted surfacing claim keys the app through `Regime.requires` predicates evaluated on the app's context at decision time; a marker recorded "after the run" (the signal) can never key one. Per-decision snapshots multiply stage recomputation. Revision: tag each marker live or post hoc; snapshot at each fit; ship in the pilot only the readings the engine already makes plus the declared measurement markers.

8. **NHANES glucose "judged on a later cycle" has no engine path.** `SplitSpec.holdout` is a fraction (decisions.py 428-444); internal-external by cycle is cross-validation, not a seal. Needs an outside scorer or a holdout-by-column option, listed nowhere.

9. **Smaller.** Bang's population needs excluding known diabetes by code; the driver's own rule is numeric-range only (`options.CUSTOM_RANGE`). The referee's sample size is not pre-registered; planted reasons must enter the referee's view only, never the record. Blinded classification of stuck runs needs the model redacted from `run.analyst_model` and the transcript. `--marker` and `want` need a controlled vocabulary before any run. PILOT_READINESS cites a §7.6 that does not exist.

10. **Nolan's two hours are pointed at an empty set.** Nothing is promoted in the pilot. Use them for: blind-rating ten referee verdicts; a real-versus-shuffled discrimination test on theorist proposals; one walkthrough of a Haiku record.

**Smallest pilot that still decides production.** Four datasets: Bang, Sullivan, the hardened Predict fixture, the new Estimate fixture; the decoy mechanically. Haiku × 3 personas on all four (12); Sonnet × 3 on Bang, Sullivan and the Estimate fixture (9); two more Haiku per persona on the Estimate fixture (6); Opus × 3 on the Estimate fixture (3): 30 agent runs. Sweep around the default plan and one plan per model per dataset; default and random paths everywhere. One theorist pass on the real and the shuffled corpus, scored for overlap and routing. This decides the model (cost, faithfulness, robustness, Estimate-fixture bias and coverage), whether personas raise coverage on real data, whether agents beat the mechanical arms per cost, and yields the first surfacing evidence: the materiality distribution on two real tables.

**Cut, stage, defer.** Defer NHANES glucose until rebuilt with a later-cycle scorer; defer the measurement fixture until its truth is designed; defer the Bang replication sweep or add it to §10 as an SU-A-sized package; stage markers (engine-read first); move the held-out generator shelf and the real false-promotion measurement after the pilot; cut Opus on the Predict fixture.

**Questions only Nolan can answer.** Which later NHANES cycle counts, and is 2021–2023's changed design acceptable? Does he accept that the pilot's claim cycle yields proposals and a prior-driven share, not a false-promotion rate? Is a 30-run pilot enough to commit a production model, or does he want the fixtures' tie broken by cost alone? What is the Decide cap per journey?

**Least certain judgments.** (1) That a Predict shelf fit on Bang is long enough to make the full sweep infeasible; I have no measured Bang fit. (2) That the Predict fixture cannot discriminate models; a persona that drops predictors or chooses a poor family might show regret I did not estimate. (3) That the registry's `judgment` self-grade makes "reach a grade" vacuous as the shuffled control would be scored; the scoring script may already mean a higher grade.

**Sound, keep.** The driver's refusals and neutral run labels; the record forbidding validity and unknown fields; the dry run's outcomes written before it ran (18 of 18 agree); the surfacing/choice distinction and "surfacing first"; the engine freeze; the scripted decision rule; replication as the SU-A sweep with analysts as the many-analysts arm; the corpus kept outside the registry.
