Missing predictor values

How should rows with missing predictor values be handled?

The answer depends on the purpose: inference imputes with the outcome, prediction without it.

No predictor has a blank cell.
  • Multiple imputationWhere a predictor has a blank to impute, several copies of the data are imputed with the outcome, and the coefficients are pooled across them by Rubin's rules.more where over 20% of the rows have a blank to impute
  • Complete casesRows missing any predictor are dropped; the participant flow shows how many.
  • Fill in each foldUnder prediction: blanks filled in each training fold without the outcome; every row stays.
