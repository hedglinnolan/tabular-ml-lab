How should nutrient intakes be adjusted for total energy?

People who eat more eat more of everything; each method answers a different question about a nutrient.

protein_g tracks energy_kcal most closely.
  • Residual, energy keptusualEach nutrient's residual on energy, energy kept: the standard model's swap, in its units.
  • Standard modelEnergy stays in the model: more of the nutrient in place of other calories.
  • Residual, energy droppedEach nutrient's residual on energy, energy dropped: differs when covariates track energy.
  • Density plus energyNutrient per calorie, energy its own term: obscure, and still biased (Tomova 2022).
  • Density aloneNutrient per calorie, energy dropped: a rescaled effect whose meaning is obscure.
  • Energy partitionCalories from the nutrient and from everything else: adding calories, not substituting.
  • All componentsnot applicableThe all-components model gives every energy source its own term, and carbohydrate and fat are not among the chosen nutrients.
  • No adjustmentTotal energy leaves the model: absolute intake, mixed with how much people eat.
