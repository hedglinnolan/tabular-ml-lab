The form of each continuous term

How does the exposure, and each continuous confounder, enter the model?

Declared before estimates, on the final scale: a spline by default, k by Harrell's rule, quintiles reported beside it.

  • age (confounder) on its raw scale, per unit of age.
  • income (confounder) on its raw scale, per unit of income.
  • fiber (confounder) on its raw scale, per unit of fiber.
  • heavy_user: 2 distinct values: it enters as recorded
  • smoker: 2 distinct values: it enters as recorded
  • Quintiles are produced beside the exposure's spline, their boundaries and reference stated, with the p for linear trend (customary).
