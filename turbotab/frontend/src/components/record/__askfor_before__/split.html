The seal: held-out rowsCONVENTION

How many rows should be sealed for one final, untouched score?

Sealed rows are scored once, when you open the seal at the end; every choice is tuned by cross-validation on the rest.

The sealOne row per unit. Held out by row: every person_id appears once, so each row is a different unit.
  • Cross-validation onlyEvery row trains and is scored by cross-validation; no untouched final score.Under inference every analyzed row estimates the coefficients, so no holdout comes first: a holdout is a prediction concept (Shmueli 2010), and one stays on offer for a final prediction score; it does not change the estimates.
  • Hold out 20%About 300 held-out rows: R² known to about ±0.08.
  • Hold out 10%About 150 held-out rows: R² known to about ±0.11.
  • Hold out 30%About 450 held-out rows: R² known to about ±0.07.
  • Hold out by cut-pointRows at or after a time value you name are sealed, row by row.
