# `clinical_longitudinal.csv` — expected findings

**600 rows × 13 columns.** 200 people × 3 scheduled visits. `subject_id`,
`visit`, `visit_date`, `age`, `sex`, `height_cm`, `weight_kg`, `sbp`, `dbp`,
`heart_rate`, `glucose`, `hba1c`, and the outcome `progressed` (binary, measured
at every visit).

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::clinical`, seed `90210`.

This is the **mirror image of `dietary_recalls.csv`**: same grain answer, opposite
reading of what the repeats are, opposite aggregation advice. A pack or a router
that treats the two alike has failed on both.

---

## The shape, measured

| Property | Value | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| Rows per person | exactly 3 | grain = repeat |
| Within-person gap between visits | 80–100 days, median 90, **CV 0.051** | a schedule — regular, with meaningful spacing |
| People whose `progressed` varies across visits | 127 of 200 | the outcome is measured per visit |
| Impossible `dbp` | 4 cells at 0 | below the floor of 15 mmHg |
| Impossible `sbp` | 3 cells at 400 / 999 | above the ceiling of 300 mmHg |
| Impossible `weight_kg` | 2 cells at 0.0 | below the floor of 0.4 kg |
| Impossible `glucose` | 2 cells at 5,000 | above the ceiling of 2,000 mg/dL |
| Impossible `height_cm` | 3 cells at 0 / 1.7 | below the floor of 20 cm |

14 impossible cells on 14 distinct rows, across 5 columns. Every one is outside
`ml/physiology_reference.py`'s **impossibility band**, so each earns a repair
proposal rather than an advisory. The reference-interval tier separately flags
`height_cm`, `weight_kg`, `sbp` and `dbp`, and that is a different claim.

## Must surface

1. **Grain = repeat**, `subject_id` offered first by the suggestion.
2. **Time points — *stated*, not asked.** The evidence is the schedule: three
   visits about 90 days apart, CV 0.051. Overturnable, with **Ask me anyway**.
3. **Averaging is NOT recommended.** A trajectory averaged is a trajectory
   destroyed. The v1 menu offers baseline / first / last / change from baseline,
   asked, with **no recommended default** — which is itself the finding.
4. **The temporal-prediction question fires**, and only here of the five
   fixtures: time points survive as rows. Yes routes to the chronological split
   `ml/splits.py` already carries, grouped as well because people repeat.
5. **The impossibility pass runs pre-seal**, on all 14 cells, before the seal is
   drawn. This is constitution §01: a stratified or grouped split computed over
   corrupted values is a worse split, and impossible entries are normally an
   exclusion that changes N.
6. **`progressed` varies within a person**, so combining a person's rows requires
   deciding *which* outcome. This is why target precedes aggregation
   (`OPENING_SEQUENCE.md` §01) and it is the concrete case for it.

---

## Must NOT surface

- **No mean-is-recommended sentence.** That belongs to `dietary_recalls.csv` and
  is wrong here. The two fixtures exist as a pair for this reason.
- **No impossibility repair applied automatically.** Setting an impossible value
  to missing is row-local and leaks nothing, but it is still a CHOICE: the
  engine may do the work, the human confirms (`DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §09).
- **No impossibility pass after the seal.** Classic runs it at `pages/02` and its
  row-dropping form at `pages/05`, both post-seal — `STATE-101`, `STATE-102`.
  Guided must not reproduce that ordering.
- **No informative-missingness prior applied without asking.** The clinical pack
  supplies the prior — missingness here often means *not ordered* — and the
  mechanism question still asks. This fixture has **no blanks at all**, so
  nothing should be routed.
- **No detection-limit reading of anything.** That prior runs in the opposite
  direction and belongs to metabolomics.
