# `genomics_expression.csv` — expected findings

**60 rows × 500 columns.** `sample_id`, `batch` (B1/B2/B3, 20 each), `sex`,
`age`, 495 raw count columns `gene_0001`…`gene_0495`, and the outcome
`condition` (case/control, 10 each per batch).

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::genomics`, seed `31337`.

The genomics pack is **deliberately thin**, and that is a design decision rather
than a shortcut (`DOMAIN_PACKS.md` §07). This fixture is built to make the thin
pack's three jobs checkable and to make a thick pack's overreach visible.

---

## The shape, measured

| Property | Value | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| p / n | **8.25** (495 features, 60 samples) | well past where an unregularized fit is degenerate |
| Counts are integers | yes, all 495 columns | counts, not concentrations |
| Library size range | 19,479 – 60,312 | a 3.1× spread across samples |
| Library size by batch (median) | B1 32,200 · B2 37,403 · B3 32,223 | it varies **with batch**, which is why normalization and batch correction are not independent choices |
| Genes with max count < 10 | 119 of 495 | low-expression genes, and the source of the false alarm below |
| Differentially expressed genes | ~6% | there is real signal to find |

---

## Must surface

1. **The wide shape is expected.** 495 features across 60 samples is an
   expression matrix, not a malformed file.
2. **p ≫ n priors on model ranking.** Regularized linear models rank above tree
   ensembles and far above anything distance-based; the shelf is **ordered**,
   never filtered (`PRODUCT_VISION.md`'s ladder puts model choice on the bottom
   rung — rank and state the concern).
3. **These are counts.** Integer-valued, variance growing with the mean. A
   Gaussian model of raw counts is wrong before any normalization question is
   reached, and the low-count genes are low counts rather than coded values.

---

## Must NOT surface

- **No normalization default, asserted or applied.** CPM, TPM and VST answer
  different questions and are not interchangeable; the choice depends on the
  assay and the question. **A pack that guesses here is the confidently-wrong
  failure `DOMAIN_PACKS.md` exists to prevent**, and this is the one place the
  correct behavior is to decline.

  A drive that sees the app pick one — or silently apply one — has found a bug,
  and it is the most serious bug this fixture can produce.
- **No batch correction applied.** `batch` exists and library size varies with
  it. Detection is legitimate; correction alters every value and is never
  automatic.
- **No log transform defaulted.** That is the metabolomics prior, and it is
  `derived` there because concentrations are log-normal *by construction*. Counts
  are not concentrations, and the argument does not transfer.
- **No grain question answered for the user.** One row per sample. See the false
  alarm below: the suggestion is polluted, and the app must still ask.
- **No models refused.** A tree ensemble at p ≫ n gets a stated concern, not a
  refusal — a competent researcher can have a reason.

---

## False alarms this fixture reproduces on purpose

| Finding | Count | Severity | What should happen |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sentinel_missing__gene_XXXX` — *"may use numeric codes for missing values"* | 10 | **critical** | Reframed under the genomics lens: these are counts, and a 7 in a low-expression gene is a 7. |
| `wide_repeated_measures` — 5 groups, proposing `melt_repeated` | 1 | info | Reframed: `gene_0001` and `gene_0002` are different genes. |
| Grain suggestion offers `gene_0153`, `gene_0105`, … | — | — | The question must still be **asked**. The suggestion is a suggestion. |

### Why the sentinel finding fires

`check_numeric_sentinels` treats an integral column with few distinct values as
a **coded** variable, which makes single-digit sentinels such as 7, 8 and 9
credible candidates. 119 of the 495 genes have a maximum count below 10, so they
look exactly like short integer scales. Ten of them clear the distance test and
are reported at `critical`.

This is a real reading failure on real data — the detector is correct about
*shape* and wrong about *kind* — and it is a clean demonstration that the lens
changes the answers rather than the questions. Nothing new is asked; ten
criticals become a stated fact.

### Why the grain suggestion is polluted

`repetition_evidence` is name-blind by design (`turbotab/grain.py`) and looks for
columns whose values repeat like a roster. A low-expression count column with
30 distinct values across 60 rows has exactly that shape. The suggestion offering
`gene_0153` is the heuristic working as specified and being useless — which is
the argument for asking rather than inferring, arriving from a direction
constitution §02 did not anticipate.
