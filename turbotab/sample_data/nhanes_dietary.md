# `nhanes_dietary.csv` — expected findings

**120 rows × 10 columns.** `SEQN`, five `DR1T*` nutrients in grams, `WTDRD1`,
`WTMEC2YR`, `SDMVSTRA`, `SDMVPSU`.

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::nhanes_dietary`, seed `7`.

**It exists to trigger detectors, not to be analyzed.** `dietary_recalls.csv`
is the fixture the drive walks end to end; this one and its sibling
`nhanes_partial_design.csv` carry the NHANES survey-design variables that
`dietary_recalls.csv` does not, so the three design detectors wired to the
dietary pack at `GUIDED-058` have something to fire on. The key-match test
binds a `LooksFor` phrase to a finding id **by running the detectors**, so a
capability with no fixture is a capability the hover cannot promise.

---

## The shape, measured

| Property | Value | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| Rows | 120 | small enough to load instantly; nothing here needs power |
| `DR1TKCAL` vs `4P + 4C + 9F + 7A` | consistent | so `atwater_finding` stays **silent**; that promise is already kept by `dietary_recalls.csv` |
| `WTDRD1` and `WTMEC2YR` | both present | the dietary weight beside the examination weight, which is the finding |
| `SDMVSTRA` × `SDMVPSU` | complete | so the design is **not** partially specified |
| stratum `999` | 9 rows, all `SDMVPSU = 1` | one stratum, one PSU — the variance contribution is undefined rather than small |

## Must surface

- `pack::dietary::survey_weights` — *use the dietary weights, not the
  examination weight.* Both are present, which is what makes the distinction
  worth stating.
- `pack::dietary::lonely_psu` — `critical`. Stratum `999` holds a single
  primary sampling unit, so Taylor-series linearization has no between-PSU
  spread to estimate its variance from.

## Must NOT surface

- `pack::dietary::partial_design` — the design is complete. That reading lives
  on `nhanes_partial_design.csv`, and the two preconditions are exact
  negations, which is why there are two files.
- `pack::dietary::atwater` — the units are internally consistent.
