# `dietary_recalls.csv` — expected findings

**600 rows × 17 columns.** 300 people × 2 twenty-four-hour recalls.
`participant_id`, `recall_number`, `recall_date`, `age`, `sex`, `bmi`,
`energy_kcal`, four `*_pct_kcal` macronutrient shares, three `*_g` amounts,
`fiber_g`, `sodium_mg`, and the outcome `hba1c`.

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::dietary`, seed `4242`.

**This is the fixture the drive report walks end to end.** It is the one that
fires the most of the opening sequence: six questions, one stated, two skipped.

---

## The shape, measured

| Property | Value | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| Rows per person | exactly 2 | the grain answer is *people repeat* |
| Within-person gap between recalls | 3–14 days, median 9, CV 0.42 | irregular and short — **not a schedule** |
| Macronutrient percentages sum to | 99.99 – 100.01 | compositional, by construction |
| corr(protein %, carbohydrate %) | −0.45 | the negative bias parts of a whole always show |
| `energy_kcal` below 500 | 12 rows | seeded under-reports, 240–430 |
| `energy_kcal` above 5,000 | 8 rows | seeded over-reports, 6,100–7,900 |
| `energy_kcal` outside the reference interval 800–4,500 | 45 rows | the 20 seeded ones plus a natural tail |
| `hba1c` distinct values per person | exactly 1 | measured once at the clinic, not per recall |

`hba1c` being constant within a person is deliberate and it is the **easy** case:
the outcome does not vary across the rows being combined, so aggregation raises
no *"which outcome?"* question. `clinical_longitudinal.csv` is the hard case.

---

## Must surface

1. **Grain = repeat.** `participant_id` is offered first by the suggestion, and
   is the only candidate the name heuristic recognizes. Answering *"one row per
   person"* here must raise the contradiction interruption.
2. **Repeats, not time points — *stated*, not asked.** The evidence is the
   spacing: gaps of 3 to 14 days, irregular, forming no schedule. Rendered as a
   skip with **Ask me anyway**, per `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §09.
3. **Unit of analysis is asked, with no default.** Two honest options, and they
   lead to different analyses.
4. **Mean recommended, with the measurement-error reason.** Not "mean is the
   default" — *"using their mean rather than a single day reduces the
   within-person measurement error that attenuates diet–outcome associations."*
   This is `derived` confidence: it is measurement-error reduction, not
   information loss.
5. **The macronutrient columns are compositional.** Four columns summing to a
   constant; correlation between parts of a whole is negatively biased by
   construction. This **gates the collinearity figure** rather than adding a
   step (`DOMAIN_PACKS.md` §07).
6. **An implausible-intake criterion is offered.** `offered`, never a default,
   never a silent filter: it changes N, so it is an eligibility criterion the
   user states, reported in participant flow.
7. **Energy adjustment is required.** Every nutrient association is confounded
   by total intake; that the adjustment is needed is not in dispute (`derived`).
   The residual method is the default *form* and is stated as `convention`, with
   nutrient density offered beside it.

---

## Must NOT surface

- **No impossibility findings on `energy_kcal`.** Measured: 0 impossible. 257
  kcal is a *possible* day and a bad *estimate*; the impossibility band is
  100–30,000. The 45 rows outside 800–4,500 are the **advisory** tier and stay
  advisory. Confusing the two tiers would turn an eligibility decision into a
  silent repair, which is the exact inversion `DOMAIN_PACKS.md` §07 warns about.
- **No temporal-prediction question.** It fires only when time points survive as
  rows. These are repeats.
- **No aggregation question if the unit is the record.** Question 6 fires only
  when the unit of analysis is the person.
- **No log-transform default.** That is the metabolomics lens, not this one.
  Intakes are not concentrations.
- **No suggestion that averaging loses information.** It reduces measurement
  error here. The opposite sentence belongs to `clinical_longitudinal.csv`.

## What a driver sees, in order

1. Upload → the lens question. Selecting **Dietary intake** (alone, or with
   *Clinical measurements and labs* — NHANES is both).
2. Structural diagnosis. One warning: `binary_text__sex`, which is correct.
3. Target → `hba1c`, detected regression at **high** confidence.
4. Grain → *yes, people repeat*, `participant_id`.
5. Repeats or time points → **stated**, with the gap evidence, overturnable.
6. Unit of analysis → asked. No default.
7. Aggregation → only if the unit is the person. Mean recommended, with reason.
8. Eligibility → asked, with the implausible-intake criterion offered as a
   candidate and the outcome's distribution withheld.
9. **SEAL.**

## False alarms this fixture reproduces

None. Structural diagnosis produces exactly one finding, `binary_text__sex`, and
it is a correct reading.
