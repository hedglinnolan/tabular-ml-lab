# `nhanes_kilojoules.csv` — expected findings

**120 rows × 10 columns.** `SEQN`, five `DR1T*` nutrients in grams with
`DR1TKCAL` **in kilojoules**, `WTDRD1`, `WTMEC2YR`, `SDMVSTRA`, `SDMVPSU`.

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::nhanes_kilojoules`, seed `9`.

**Why it exists.** `GUIDED-068`. Once the Atwater check learned to prefer the
gram columns over the percent-of-energy columns beside them,
`dietary_recalls.csv` — which carries both — reconstructed cleanly and
**passed**, which is the correct reading and left `pack::dietary::atwater`
promised by the dietary pack's hover and emitted by no fixture. A promise
nobody keeps is the app announcing it will look for something it will not, so
the promise needed a table with a real unit error in it.
