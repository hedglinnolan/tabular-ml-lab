# `nhanes_partial_design.csv` — expected findings

**120 rows × 8 columns.** `SEQN`, five `DR1T*` nutrients in grams, `WTDRD1`,
`WTMEC2YR`. **No `SDMVSTRA` and no `SDMVPSU`** — that absence is the fixture.

Generated by `make_fixtures.py::nhanes_partial_design`, seed `8`.

The sibling of `nhanes_dietary.csv`. Two files rather than one because
`partial_design` fires when a weight has no strata or PSU and `lonely_psu`
fires when both are present and a stratum holds one: the preconditions are
exact negations, so no single table can exercise both.

---

## Must surface

- `pack::dietary::survey_weights` — the dietary weight beside the examination
  weight, same as the sibling.
- `pack::dietary::partial_design` — *weights alone correct the point estimates
  toward the population; the strata and PSU are what make the standard errors
  right.* Without them the intervals are too narrow and nothing on screen shows
  it.

## Must NOT surface

- `pack::dietary::lonely_psu` — it needs both columns to mean anything, and
  there are neither.
- `pack::dietary::atwater` — the units are internally consistent.
